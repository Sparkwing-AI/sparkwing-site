# Colours

The live site is **teal-led**: card teal `#1F8576` is the dominant surface for hero, nav, product lineup and footer. Depth teal `#1A7A6E` and Night Fern `#285A5A` add section contrast. White is for on-teal text and sparing panel tiles. Gold and amber are accents for CTAs, prices on teal, and sparks. Cream is a small touch only.

Semantic tokens live in `tokens.css` and `tokens.json`.

## Contrast pairs (WCAG 2.x)

Gold on card teal is decorative only. Use white for body copy on teal, ink on gold for CTA labels, and glade on white panels.
