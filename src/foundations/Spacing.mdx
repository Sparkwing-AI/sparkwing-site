# Spacing, radii and shadows

Base unit: {tokens.spacing.unit}. Scale tokens: `space-1` through `space-20` in `tokens.css`.

Radii: `radius-sm`, `radius-md`, `radius-lg`, `radius-pill`.

Shadows: `shadow-soft`, `shadow-card` for cards and hero logo glow tokens.
