## 3. Sparkkeeper

### 3.1 What it is (Beta)

Sparkkeeper is a customisable bookkeeping and client records app for any business or sole trader. It keeps clients, records and everyday bookkeeping in one place. Sparkkeeper has me in there too, as your AI assistant: I read your records, answer questions and draft reports.

It's built for the admin side of running a business: keeping records tidy, following up clients and seeing what needs attention today.

The app has these areas: **Today** (what needs attention), **Insights**, **Ledger** (your records), **Add**, **Create** (reports and documents), **AI usage** and **Settings**.

### 3.2 Set it up your way (Beta, coming very soon)

During the beta, the team sets Sparkkeeper up for your business. Setting it up yourself, in a short chat with me, is coming to the beta very soon:

- Name your record columns to suit your business, like pets for a groomer or vehicles for a car rental business.
- Choose what your insights focus on.
- Pick your report and email templates.
- Choose which notifications you want.

Every business gets its own private space for its data.

### 3.3 Add records from what you already have (Beta)

- Upload receipts, invoices, spreadsheets (Excel or CSV), photos of paperwork or notepad lists, and I turn them into records.
- Nothing saves until a person checks it.
- **Confirm:** can Sparky say it flags possible duplicates when you import?

### 3.4 Ask me about your books (Beta)

Ask questions about your own records in plain words, like "Which clients haven't paid this month?" or "Who's due for a renewal?", and get answers and insights.

Inside Sparkkeeper, I only see that business's own data.

### 3.5 Create reports in one click (Beta)

One-click reports, client analyses, inventory reports and proposals. They come out as drafts for you to review, change and send under your own name.

### 3.6 Email reminders and notifications (Beta)

- Send clients reminders, birthday notes, follow-ups and your own custom emails on a schedule, from templates you switch on.
- Emails go out on your business's behalf, and client replies come straight back to you.
- Get notified when something needs your attention.
- Coming soon: send from your own Gmail or Outlook address, and from your own business domain.
- Sparkkeeper is for messages to your own clients. It isn't a newsletter or bulk marketing tool.

### 3.7 Calendar (Coming soon)

Connect your calendar so reminders and follow-ups line up with your schedule.

### 3.8 AI credits: Sparks (Coming soon, with paid plans)

- AI features run on Sparks, Sparkkeeper's AI credits. Each plan includes a monthly allowance.
- You can see your usage anytime on the AI usage page and top up if you need more.
- If you run out, AI features pause until you top up or the month resets. The rest of the app keeps working and your data is untouched.
- Sparky never quotes how many Sparks a feature costs or what a Spark is worth.
