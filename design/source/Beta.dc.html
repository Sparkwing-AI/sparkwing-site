Questions about the beta

What does the beta cost?

Nothing. The beta is free, and we cover your AI credits. Seats are limited.

What happens if I run out of AI credits?

Ask us for a top-up, and we will add more where we can, at no cost to you. Until it comes through, AI features pause and everything else keeps working, including your records.

Can I keep my account after the beta?

That is the plan. Before the beta ends, we will share the plans and prices so you can choose one. If you decide not to, you can still sign in, see your records and export them, and AI features stay paused.

This is a beta, so things can go wrong. If we ever need to reset data, we will tell you as early as we can and give you the chance to export first wherever possible. Please keep your original files too.

Want it all deleted instead? Email info@sparkwing.com.au and we will remove it.

Is my data private? Will it be used to train AI?

We don't use your data to train AI models. Sparkkeeper's AI features use Claude models from Anthropic through its commercial service, and those terms bar Anthropic from training on your content.

You can use real records in the beta. Please leave out government ID numbers, card numbers and health details, as Sparkkeeper doesn't need them. By the time your beta starts, Sparkkeeper will mask customers' personal details before anything reaches the AI.

We look at how the app is used, such as which features get opened and where errors happen, so we can improve it. If something breaks, we start with system logs. If we need to see your records to fix it, we will ask you first, unless it is an urgent security problem. Our Privacy policy has the full detail.

Will you contact me for feedback?

Maybe. We may reach out to some beta users at random for a short interview, so it isn't guaranteed. You are always free to say no.

How do I send feedback or requests?

From inside the app. There is a place to send feedback and feature requests straight to our team.

Does Sparkkeeper replace my accountant or registered books?

No. It sits alongside your registered books and invoicing. AI answers are drafts, so please check them before you rely on them.

Can I leave early?

Yes, any time. Export your records if you want a copy, then email info@sparkwing.com.au and tell us whether to keep or delete your account.
