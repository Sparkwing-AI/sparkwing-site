Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Sparkwing Pty Ltd (ABN 90 703 825 370), based in Sydney, Australia, collects, uses, shares and protects personal information. It covers our website, enquiries, discovery calls and apps.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to talk with you, deliver our services and run our business.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't use client data to train AI models.
  • Some of our team and providers are outside Australia, including in the Philippines and the United States.
  • You can ask to see, fix or delete your information at info@sparkwing.com.au.

The laws we follow

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). For people in the Philippines, we also follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 where it applies to us.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email, phone number, business name and role, when you email us, fill in a form on our website, book a call or meet us at an event.
  • What you tell us: your goals and business needs, plus our notes from calls, demos and quotes.
  • App account details: your name, work email, role, sign-in details, settings, and records of how you use the app and its AI features.
  • What you put into our apps: the questions you ask and the files and records you add.
  • Billing records: business contact details, invoices and payment records.
  • Technical details: your IP address, browser, device type and the pages you request, which our hosting and security providers may log.
  • Working with us: the details we need if you are a supplier, contractor or job applicant.

We don't ask for sensitive information through our website or email. Please don't email us government ID numbers, policy numbers or health details.

You can browse our website without telling us who you are, and you can ask general questions using a pseudonym. We need your real details to give you a quote, an account or a contract.

If you don't give us the details we need, we may not be able to reply to you, prepare a quote or give you access to an app.

If we receive information we didn't ask for and don't need, we delete or de-identify it as soon as we reasonably can.

How we collect it

Most of it comes directly from you. We may also get your details from your employer or our client when they set up your app account, from someone who refers you to us, from business events, and from public business sources such as company websites. Our providers collect technical details automatically when you visit our website or use our apps.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, run discovery calls and demos, and prepare recommendations and quotes.
  • To set up, deliver and support our apps and services.
  • To keep our systems secure and to prevent misuse.
  • To understand how our services are used, and to improve them.
  • To send invoices, manage our business and meet our legal and tax duties.

Marketing emails

We don't send marketing emails or newsletters today. If you ask about our services, we'll reply and follow up with you about that, and you can tell us to stop any time.

If we start sending marketing emails or newsletters, we'll update this policy first and include an easy way to unsubscribe.

Analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how our website is used, such as the pages viewed, where visitors came from and how quickly pages load. Cloudflare says this service doesn't use cookies and doesn't track people across websites.

Our security and hosting providers may use essential cookies and logs to keep the site running safely. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when it fetches them.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels today. We'll update this policy before we add them.

How we use AI

In our apps. AI features use Claude models from Anthropic, in the United States, through its commercial service. When you use these features, your question and the business content needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic. Anthropic's commercial terms bar it from training its models on that content.

Sparkwing is designed to mask customers' personal details before anything reaches the AI, and we'll confirm what's live for your pilot.

In our business. We also use AI tools to help run Sparkwing, including to organise notes and draft documents. These tools may process what you share with us during enquiries and calls.

Decisions. AI outputs are drafts for people to review. We don't use computer programs alone to make decisions about you that could significantly affect your rights or interests.

Information our clients put into our apps

Our clients may add details about their own customers to our apps. In that case, the client decides what is collected and why, and we handle it on the client's instructions under our contract with them. We don't add those people to our marketing lists or contact them for our own purposes.

If a business that uses our apps holds your information, please contact that business first and read its privacy policy. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to the business or help it respond.

Who we share it with

  • Our team and contractors, when they need it for their work.
  • AI services: Anthropic and other AI tools we use to run our business.
  • Hosting, network and security: providers including Cloudflare, GitHub and VentraIP.
  • Email, documents and work tracking: providers including Microsoft and Atlassian.
  • Finance and professional support: our accounting software, accountants, lawyers and insurers.
  • Regulators and authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A future buyer or investor, if our business is sold or restructured, under confidentiality terms.

Sending information overseas

We are likely to disclose personal information to recipients in the Philippines, where members of our team work, and the United States, where several of our providers are based. Some providers also run global networks, so information may pass through or be stored in other countries where they operate.

We take reasonable steps to make sure overseas recipients handle personal information in line with the privacy laws that apply to us.

Storage and security

We hold information electronically with our cloud providers. We limit access to people who need it and take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No online system is perfectly secure, so please tell us straight away if you think something has gone wrong.

We keep personal information while we need it for the purposes in this policy or the law requires us to keep it, such as tax records. After that, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell the people affected and the regulator as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email info@sparkwing.com.au and we'll aim to reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

It's free to ask and free to correct your information. If giving you access takes significant work, we may charge a reasonable cost, and we'll tell you before we do.

If we can't do what you ask, we'll explain why in writing and tell you how to complain, unless the law prevents us. If we refuse a correction, you can ask us to keep a note with your information saying you think it's wrong. If we correct information we gave to another organisation, we'll tell them if you ask.

If you are in the Philippines, you also have rights under the Data Privacy Act to be informed, to object to processing, to have your data blocked or erased, to get a copy in a portable format, and to claim damages where the law allows.

Making a complaint

If you think we've mishandled your information, email info@sparkwing.com.au with the details. We'll look into it and aim to give you a written answer within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. In the Philippines, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Children

Our services are made for businesses and aren't directed at anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. You can ask us for a free copy of this policy in another format.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email info@sparkwing.com.au.
